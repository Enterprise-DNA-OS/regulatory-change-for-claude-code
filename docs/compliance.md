# Compliance checks and their limits

Checked 6 November 2026. This desk records decisions and evidence for a responsible compliance professional. It does not determine applicable law, monitor every regulator, certify compliance or submit statutory reports. Demo notices, decisions and dates are synthetic exercises.

## Workflow

SOURCE_REVIEW compares the last successful source capture date with the business review_days setting. Seven days is the demo policy, not a legal interval. IMPACT_REVIEW flags an unassessed change past its agreed review date. ACTION_EVIDENCE flags completed actions whose evidence or completion version no longer matches the change. These are local operating controls, not statutory duties.

Sign-off requires a definitive current-version assessment, a different reviewer, an implementation action for applicable changes, and completion backed by current-version evidence. A monitor decision stays open. An imported revision increments the version and invalidates earlier approval for present purposes. An operator must check the original documents. References and self-declared actor names are not identity verification or a tamper-proof audit trail.

## NZ personal information

[Privacy Principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) requires reasonable safeguards against loss, misuse and disclosure. Public database privileges are revoked, reports use private file permissions where the operating system supports them, and HTML escapes record text. These controls do not establish that a deployment meets Principle 5. Operators must set access, backups and storage security.

[Privacy Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) limits retention to the purposes for which personal information may lawfully be used. NZ_IPP9 flags personal-information evidence past its recorded retention review date. It does not select a lawful retention period or delete records. Record the purpose and a review date, assess any legal hold, and keep original evidence in an appropriately controlled store.

## Australian personal information

[APP 11 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) covers security and, subject to exceptions, destruction or de-identification when personal information is no longer needed. AU_APP11 flags overdue review dates on evidence attached to AU sources. Applicability to the business and any record retention duties require a competent human review. Source jurisdiction is only a routing aid, not a legal applicability decision.

## Interpretation

A clean check means no configured record gap was found. It says nothing about missing laws, incomplete source coverage, truthful evidence, access outside this application or obligations not configured here. No automatic retention deletion, legal opinion or regulator notification is included.
