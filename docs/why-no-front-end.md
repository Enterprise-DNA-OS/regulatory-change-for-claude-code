# The change register is the product

A weekly review needs the sources, impact decisions, actions, evidence and reasons behind sign-off. Those records fit a database. The coding agent runs the commands; a person makes and owns the decisions.

`npm run view` produces read-only branded HTML for the week and assurance checks. `npm run docs` produces impact assessment records and evidence packs. There is no app server or editing screen. Change the business name, colours and optional logo path in brand.json, then regenerate.

A screen brings mobile entry, drag-and-drop triage, permission-aware collaboration, notifications and attachment browsing. This base does not provide those experiences, a legal research library or an automatic global feed. Enterprise DNA scopes the interfaces and connections a business needs in its custom version. HTML reports are snapshots and require regeneration after changes. Keep them private.
